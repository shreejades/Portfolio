How I approach high-priority service design

Research strategy begins with framing the right questions, recruiting representative users, and validating insights with both qualitative and quantitative evidence.

  1. Define research goals and hypotheses with product, data, and strategy partners.
  2. Recruit diverse users aligned to demographic and behavioral profiles.
  3. Collect qualitative evidence through interviews, field studies and usability sessions.
  4. Triangulate findings with analytics and behavioral data.
  5. Design user journeys and wireframes grounded in evidence.
  6. Document recommendations for product teams and decision makers.
